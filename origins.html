Origins

From Web3 art platform
to AI agent identity

NexArt began in 2023 exploring digital art on Web3, with an initial concept for a curated NFT platform for female artists. Working with blockchain-based ownership and authenticity led to a broader question: as more activity is carried out by AI, how do we verify who, or what, we are dealing with?

NexArt's current concept builds on that foundation, applying blockchain-based identity to AI agents.

The digital art platform was not launched. The work below is kept as a record of that early exploration.
