About NexArt

NexArt Limited is a London-based company exploring trust and identity for AI agents. Its current focus is an early-stage concept for verifiable, tamper-evident identity for autonomous AI systems. NexArt grew out of earlier work on authenticity and ownership in digital art (see Origins).

Technology Research

NexArt's research centres on AI agent identity: how autonomous AI systems can be given verifiable, tamper-resistant identities as they increasingly act without direct human supervision.

Patent status: UK patent application No. GB 2604570.8, filed March 2026, pending.

Founder & Director

Tia Zhong is the founder and director of NexArt Limited. She developed the concept behind NexArt's UK patent application. She holds an MA in Film Studies from the University of East Anglia and an MA in Education from University College London. Her work bridges art, technology and digital identity.
