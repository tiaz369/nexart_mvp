About NexArt

NexArt Limited is a London-based technology company working at the intersection of AI infrastructure and the digital creative economy. We develop tools and protocols that address trust, identity, and ownership in decentralised digital environments — with a focus on supporting independent and female artists alongside our core technology work.

Technology Research

Our technology research centres on AI agent identity authentication: how autonomous AI systems can be assigned verifiable, tamper-resistant identities in a world where agents increasingly act without direct human supervision.
